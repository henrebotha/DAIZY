Hello! This is the web version of DAIZY PG-2:

Click Anywhere On The Screen To Start Audio!

· CHORDS: Click the center (Open) or petals to switch chords.
· STRINGS: Click the strings on the right, or use keys A, S, D, F, G, H to play notes.
· TIMBRE: Click the icons above the strings to switch sounds.
· CONTROLS: Use CAPO to transpose. Turn on STRUM to auto-strum. Drag knobs to adjust DECAY & SUSTAIN.
· AMBIENCE：Click 🔊 to play the scene's ambient sound, and use the Vol knob to control the volume.
· MIDI: Connect your DAIZY PG-2 to play along!
